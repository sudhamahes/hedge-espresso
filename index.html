The local

A café worth
walking to.

We’re on the corner of Lillian Ave in Salisbury, a few steps from the 125 stop. Some people find us by accident. Most of them come back the next morning.

We open at six because plenty of you start early. Breakfast runs until eleven, lunch picks up from there, and the cabinet is full of sweet things all day. Free-range eggs, and most of the menu can be made gluten-free, dairy-free or vegan. The eggs benedict is the one everyone orders, and when Brisbane turns on a cold morning there are blankets on the chairs. Order at the counter, we’ll have it out to you quickly, and we’ll remember it next time.
